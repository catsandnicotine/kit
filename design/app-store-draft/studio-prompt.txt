Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: private art-direction concept for a connected three-panel App Store screenshot set, not a final published marketing asset.
Primary request: A restrained, photorealistic studio product photograph composition of three iPhone 17 phones displaying the supplied Kit app screens. Give the actual devices believable three-dimensional depth, only slight natural tilt, realistic glass and aluminum, soft side lighting and contact shadows on a seamless warm ivory studio cyclorama. Sophisticated, quiet and minimal.
Input images: Image 1 is the exact app screen for the first phone (question and green pixel eye); Image 2 is the exact app screen for the second phone (answer and four recall icons); Image 3 is the exact app screen for the third phone (text and photo card editor). These are supporting screen-content references, not phone-hardware references.
Composition: landscape panorama approximately 1.38:1, designed to split into three equal tall portrait panels. One complete, large phone centred within each third, same scale; no phone crosses a panel boundary. Front displays remain easily legible. Subtle varied 3 to 6 degree lean, shallow perspective rather than extreme angles. Phones occupy roughly the lower 80% of each panel. Consistent studio floor and illumination connect all three. Leave a little quiet space above. Accurate iPhone 17 front proportions and Dynamic Island, delicate black/silver physical edges, no invented rear camera or oversized bezels. Devices rest naturally on the surface, not levitating.
Screen constraints: preserve each supplied UI, its text, green eye, warm coral arrow, muted blue anvil and green checkmarks as faithfully as possible. No invented features, no new logos, no added screen art. Screen content should remain sharp with very gentle realistic glass reflection that does not obscure it.
Text: no added headlines, slogans, captions, labels, watermark or external text.
Avoid: floating fantasy devices, neon glows, busy religious props, giant crosses, extra icons, gold ornament, cartoon phones, dramatic perspective, excessive reflections, excessive shadows, warped or retyped UI.
