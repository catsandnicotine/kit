Art & symbols

What does chiaroscuro describe?

Imagine a face lit by one small window. The forehead catches the light; the opposite cheek disappears into shadow.

The light and dark areas give the face weight, depth and a sense of space.

This contrast appears throughout painting, drawing and photography. It can be gentle, or almost theatrical.

What is the relationship between those light and dark areas called?

Light and shadow.

Their contrast creates depth and form.
