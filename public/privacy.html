Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

Kit does not require a Kit account or send your study data to a Kit server. It has no built-in analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data Storage

Your decks, cards, study progress, review history, and media are saved on your device. Kit uses local databases and files for this storage.

iCloud Sync

On supported Apple devices, Kit uses your personal iCloud Drive for supported deck and progress syncing when iCloud is available. Kit reads and writes its files there; the developer does not receive your study data. Imported pictures and audio are currently stored on the importing device.

Imported Content

Imported decks are saved locally. If you choose to share or export a deck, its contents go to the destination you select. Imported cards may contain external links or remote media; opening those can contact the provider.

No Third-Party Services

Kit does not use any third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking libraries. The app operates entirely offline for its core functionality.

Children

Kit does not ask for age information or create user profiles, including profiles for children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted here.
