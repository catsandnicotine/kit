Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Overview

Kit does not require a Kit account or send your study data to a Kit server. It has no built-in analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data Storage

Your decks, cards, study progress, review history, answer-reveal counts, and media are saved on your device. Kit uses local databases and files for this storage.

iCloud Sync

On supported Apple devices, Kit uses your personal iCloud Drive for supported deck, progress, and answer-reveal count syncing when iCloud is available. Kit reads and writes its files there; the developer does not receive your study data. Imported pictures, audio, and videos are currently stored on the importing device.

Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Clipboard

You can choose media for a card, take a photo, or record a video using the system picker. Camera and microphone access are requested only when needed for capture, and can be changed in iOS Settings. Selected media is saved with your deck. Kit reads the clipboard when you choose to paste into a card; it does not monitor your clipboard in the background.

Imported Content

Imported decks are saved locally. If you choose to share or export a deck, its contents go to the destination you select. Imported cards may contain external links or remote media. Viewing a card with remote media can contact its provider automatically; opening an external link also contacts that provider.

Retention and Deletion

Kit retains your study data for use in the app. Deleting a deck removes it from your library and attempts to clean up its local files and related iCloud files. This does not guarantee that every stored copy is erased: failed cleanup, offline devices, retained sync-recovery files, saved exports, and device or cloud backups may leave copies. You can manage those copies through the relevant device, Files app, or backup service. Removing Kit does not necessarily remove its iCloud files.

The developer cannot retrieve or delete your private study files for you because Kit does not upload them to a developer-operated service. Support messages you choose to send by email are received by the developer and the email provider; do not attach private decks unless you want to share them for support.

No Third-Party Services

Kit does not use any third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking libraries. The app operates entirely offline for its core functionality.

Children

Kit does not ask for age information or create user profiles, including profiles for children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted here.
