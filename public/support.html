Kit support

Flashcards, kept simple.

Start a deck

Use + in the library to make cards or import an Anki .apkg file. Each side can contain text and photos. Save a card when both sides have content.

Edit a card

Press and hold the side you want to change, then choose Edit this side. Card content changes only when you save. The separate, confirmed answer-count reset takes effect immediately and leaves the review schedule unchanged. The small card-face controls let you return to the question without advancing.

Missing pictures or sound

Use the deck's ··· control to open Manage deck, then its Deck details ··· control. Choose Restore pictures & audio and select the original Anki package. Restoration adds media without replacing your cards or study history. Some Anki templates and add-ons are not supported; imported JavaScript and inline script actions do not run inside Kit.

Keep a copy

In Manage deck → Deck details → Export, choose Cards only to share a fresh copy, or Cards & study history to carry Kit scheduling and review history into another Kit installation. Keep the exported file somewhere you can find it.

An export is a snapshot of the current card faces. It does not preserve editable Anki note templates, Kit answer-reveal counts, or every app preference. Your original Anki package stays unchanged, but it does not contain later edits or progress made in Kit.

Offline and iCloud

Saved decks work offline. Supported deck data and progress can use your own iCloud Drive when it is available. Imported pictures, sound, and videos currently stay on the device where they were added. Kit does not connect to AnkiWeb or synchronize with desktop Anki.

Get help

Email jikelvin9@gmail.com with what happened, your Kit version from Settings, and your iOS version. For a crash, include the steps just before it closed. Please leave out private study material unless it is needed and you choose to share it.

The privacy policy is also available in Kit's Settings.
